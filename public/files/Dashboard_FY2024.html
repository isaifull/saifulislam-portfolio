Segregation is the leverage point, not tonnage: every kilogram shifted out of the incineration stream into recycling avoids 0.7 kgCO₂e, and avoided procurement of single-use items removes emissions upstream of the waste stream entirely.

Read the inventory before you trust it

The 74% Scope 2 figure is a data artefact as much as a finding. Procurement (extra supply chain), commuting, travel, inhalers, fleet and refrigerants were all marked not estimated. Globally, supply chain is ~71% of healthcare emissions; here it is 0%. That does not mean this hospital buys less — it means the largest Scope 3 block was never measured. Treat 9,419.9 t as a floor, not the full footprint.

Method & boundaries

Design choiceWhat was applied
ApproachBottom-up, activity data × emission factor, per GHG Protocol and HCWH Climate Impact Checkup
Emission factorsGrid 0.727 kgCO₂e/kWh (CEA Baseline v20.0, Dec 2024); diesel 2.68 kg/L; LPG 2.983 kg/kg; desflurane GWP 2,540; isoflurane 510; waste incineration 0.7 kg/kg (CPCB 2023)
Anaesthetic activity data130 kg CO₂ (~66,327 L) and 46 kg N₂O (~24,892 L) consumed in the reporting period
Derived electricity7,004.95 t ÷ 0.727 kg/kWh ≈ 9,635 MWh annual consumption (implied, not metered in the report)
Intensity denominators3,24,690 patient encounters (29.0 kgCO₂e each); 900 beds (10.5 tCO₂e/bed); 56,868.7 m² (0.166 tCO₂e/m²)
Known limitationsSingle-year snapshot, no base-year trend, no uncertainty or assurance, six Scope 3 categories unquantified, refrigerant and fleet emissions absent
